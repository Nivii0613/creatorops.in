Frequently asked questions

Early answers to the most common questions about CreatorOps and what the beta includes.

Is CreatorOps live?

CreatorOps is currently in beta. The goal right now is to work closely with early users and shape the workflow around real creator problems.

Who is it for?

Creators who manage paid collaborations and want a more organized system for deals, invoices, contracts, and payments.

Do I need GST to use it?

No. Many creators start without GST registration, so early workflows can support simple invoices and basic payment tracking too.

Can brands use it too?

Yes. Brands and agencies can join the beta interest list, but the initial product direction is creator-first.
